SELLER STUDIO · KGM THRIFT EMPORIUM

Privacy notice

Effective October 27, 2026

Seller Studio is a private seller dashboard operated by Keith Marin for KGM Thrift Emporium. It runs locally on the owner's computer. This notice covers eBay account reads, authorized Facebook Page posting, and the planned Pinterest publishing integration. Pinterest publishing is not yet implemented or connected.

Information used

With the owner's authorization, Seller Studio reads eBay inventory, active listing IDs, titles, descriptions, photos, prices, quantities, listing statuses, and order information for reporting. Optional eBay traffic and transaction evidence reads require additional authorization. Raw order responses may contain buyer information in server memory; the dashboard does not intentionally retain buyer names or addresses on disk or share them with social platforms.

Social publishing

When enabled, Facebook automation sends the owner's public listing photo URL, title, price and eBay listing link to the authorized Facebook Page. Facebook retrieves the image and hosts the resulting content. The dashboard tracks posts to prevent duplicates and, when cleanup is enabled, deletes its own recorded posts after eBay confirms the listings have ended.

The planned Pinterest feature will require separate authorization to read the owner's boards, create Pins containing public product photos, titles, descriptions and listing links, and read or delete the app's Pins for tracking and optional cleanup. It will operate on the owner's selected board. Pinterest access and publishing will begin only after the integration is implemented and authorized. The application does not request buyer data for social publishing or use account data to train AI models.

Storage and retention

Application credentials are stored in the owner's local configuration. eBay authorization tokens and the Facebook Page token can be saved in macOS Keychain; the application has no plaintext token-file fallback. Planned Pinterest authorization will likewise use Keychain. Credentials are not included in this public site.

Fetched business records remain in the active dashboard's memory and are cleared on restart or disconnect. Local Facebook delivery history retains listing IDs, Facebook post IDs, delivery states, automation preferences and a hashed connection identifier until the owner removes it. This history prevents duplicate posts and supports cleanup; it contains no buyer details or listing photos. An archived database may contain older sample data and manually created drafts. Planned Pinterest history will contain corresponding board, Pin and listing identifiers and delivery states, rather than buyer information.

Service providers

Authorized data is exchanged with eBay, Meta/Facebook and, once connected, Pinterest for the purposes above. These providers process information under their own policies. Social posts may be public and can be saved or shared by others. Seller Studio does not sell account information. OpenAI integration is disabled; the dashboard does not send photos or account records to OpenAI.

This website

This static notice is hosted on GitHub Pages without analytics, advertising, external fonts or third-party scripts. GitHub may process network request information under its privacy statement. The separate eBay authorization return page receives a temporary code and state in its URL; the hosting provider receives that request. The page does not exchange codes for tokens. Never share authorization URLs publicly.

Controls and deletion

The owner can pause automatic posting and turn cleanup off in the dashboard. Disconnect and forget removes the relevant saved Keychain authorization and active connection; reported Keychain failures require manual removal. Authorization can also be revoked through the connected platform's account settings. Disconnecting does not automatically delete published posts or local duplicate-prevention history. The owner can delete published posts through Facebook or Pinterest and remove local history files and archived drafts on the owner's computer. Deleting history resets duplicate protection. Hosting logs and content saved by others are controlled by their respective providers.

Contact

For privacy or deletion questions, contact KGM Thrift Emporium through our eBay store. Do not send passwords, access tokens or buyer information in public posts or repository issues.
